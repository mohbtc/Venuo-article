01 / THE PROBLEM

The blockchain remembers everything.

That transparency is one of the reasons blockchains are powerful. Transactions can be verified, balances can be tracked, and activity can be audited without relying on a central authority.

But there’s another side to that transparency.

Once something happens onchain, it can leave a permanent trail.

Wallet activity, transaction history, counterparties, and financial behavior can become visible to anyone willing to look closely enough.

THE QUESTION How do you preserve the verifiability of a public network without making every detail public?

This is the problem Venuo is exploring.

02 / THE APPROACH

Privacy doesn't mean disappearing.

The interesting part of Venuo is that it doesn't treat privacy and verification as opposites.

Instead, sensitive information can stay private while the network still receives the proof it needs to verify what happened.
